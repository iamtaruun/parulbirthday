For my Doolu,

Happy Birthday, Parul.

I still remember 9th class — the first time I actually noticed you. We had talked before that, but never really talked. Then 21 December happened, that one conversation on Instagram, and somehow I never stopped.

You're the most dramatic, most stubborn, most Mirchi person I know, and I wouldn't change a single bit of it. When you get angry I call you Doolu on purpose, because I know you'll pretend to stay mad for exactly four more minutes and then laugh.

Thank you for the late night calls, the pointless fights, the random voice notes, the photos I keep even when you say you look bad in them. Thank you for being the person I want to tell everything to first.

I'm not great with big poetic words, so here's the simple version: my life got softer after you. You make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering, Paaro.

This year, I hope you get everything you're too shy to ask for. And I hope you always know that whatever happens, I'm choosing you. Again and again.

Happy Birthday, Cutepiee.
